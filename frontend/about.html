Who We Are

Driven by Passion for Auto Care

AutoTrack was founded with a simple mission: make vehicle maintenance management accessible, intuitive, and powerful for every car owner in Sri Lanka and beyond.

We combine cutting-edge technology with deep automotive expertise to deliver a platform that helps you stay on top of your vehicle's health, never miss a service, and save money through proactive maintenance.

Our team of engineers and automotive specialists work tirelessly to ensure AutoTrack remains the most reliable and feature-rich platform for vehicle owners and workshop managers alike.

  • Founded in 2024 in Colombo, Sri Lanka
  • Serving 500+ vehicle owners
  • 12,000+ services tracked to date
  • 24/7 customer support
  • ISO 27001 certified data security
